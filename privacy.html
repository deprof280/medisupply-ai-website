MEDISUPPLY HUB

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 29 October 2026

This policy explains how MediSupply Hub handles information when you visit our website or contact us through the website assistant or WhatsApp. It applies to information collected through those channels and related customer-support workflows.

Information we receive

Depending on how you contact us, we may receive:

  • Your name, email address and phone number if you choose to provide them.
  • The messages, product questions, quotation requests and other details you submit.
  • A chat session identifier used to keep a conversation together and recognise a returning browser session.
  • Basic technical information processed by our website, hosting and communications providers to deliver and protect the service.

Information saved in your browser

The website assistant uses your browser’s local storage to remember its chat session identifier and any name, email address or phone number you enter. This lets the assistant retain those details for a later visit from the same browser. This information remains there until it is cleared from your browser storage. You can remove it using your browser’s site-data or storage settings.

How we use information

We use the information above to answer questions, discuss products, prepare or follow up on quotation requests, provide customer support, maintain conversation continuity and operate the website and its support workflows. The website assistant uses AI to help generate responses; a response may be routed for human follow-up where the workflow supports it.

Service providers and sharing

Information is processed by the services needed to operate the channels you use. These may include our website hosting provider, the n8n automation workflow used for support messages, and Meta when you communicate with us through WhatsApp. Our current self-hosted n8n setup uses an internet tunnel to receive webhook traffic; the tunnel provider may process connection data while routing requests. The workflow may also send information to other services if they are configured in it.

These providers process information to deliver their services and under their own privacy terms. We do not use this policy to promise a particular provider’s storage location or retention period.

Retention

We keep information for as long as reasonably needed to respond to you, support our business operations and meet applicable obligations. Chat messages and related records may be retained by the n8n workflow or connected services according to their current configuration. Browser-saved details remain until you clear them. Contact us if you would like us to review a request to access, correct or delete information, subject to applicable requirements and technical limitations.

Your choices and requests

You can choose not to provide optional contact details, clear the assistant’s saved details from your browser, or stop using the website assistant or WhatsApp channel. To ask a privacy question or make a request about information you have submitted, use the contact section on our website and identify the channel and approximate date of your conversation. We may need enough information to locate the relevant record and verify your request.

Security and sensitive information

Information sent or stored online cannot be guaranteed completely secure. Please do not send patient-identifiable details, medical records, payment-card details, passwords or other sensitive information through the general website chat or WhatsApp support. The assistant is for product and customer-service questions, not medical advice or emergency care.

Children and changes to this policy

Our website and support channels are intended for people seeking business or product information, not for children. We may update this policy as our website, services or legal obligations change. The effective date above indicates the latest version published here.

Contact MediSupply Hub

For privacy questions or requests, use the website’s contact section or message us through the support channel you used to contact us.
